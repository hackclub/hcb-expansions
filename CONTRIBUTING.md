# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to add a phrase! Here's what we look for, so your PR can get merged quickly.

## Some context first

HCB is the platform Hack Club runs to help student hackathons, clubs, and nonprofits raise and manage money. The phrases in [`phrases.txt`](phrases.txt) rotate on the HCB homepage, which is where teenagers, their parents, teachers, and donors land when they're deciding whether to trust us with real money.

So here's the test we use: **could a teacher read your phrase out loud to a class without having to explain it or apologize for it?** If the answer is yes, you're probably good.

## Adding yours

Open `phrases.txt` and scroll through it first. It's short, and it'll give you a better feel for the style than anything written here. Then add your phrase on a new line at the very bottom and open a PR.

Please only add lines. Don't edit, sort, or remove anyone else's, and don't touch other files. Those PRs need a closer look and will sit for a lot longer.

## The format

The main words start with H, C, and B, in that order: `Hungry Cheese Bandit`. If you need an extra word, put it in parentheses, like `Here Comes (the) Bride` or `Head of Cash (and) Budgeting`. Small words like "of" or "for" can stay bare if it reads better (`Happy Camels on Bicycles`), and splitting a word with a capital letter works too (`Happy CapyBaras`).

Use Title Case, and feel free to add punctuation if it helps the joke land. `Help! Coyote Bear!` just wouldn't be the same without it. Watch for stray spaces at the end of your line.

## What we love

The best phrases make you smile the moment you read them. Nobody seeing yours on the homepage knows who you are or what you had in mind, so the joke has to work all on its own.

A few of our favourites from the list:

- `Highly Confused Butler`
- `Hyper Caffeinated Bears`
- `Haunted Castle Bats`
- `Hopefully Correct Balances?`
- `Human Code Bug`

Silly and wholesome usually wins. Food, animals, coding, building things, and gentle jokes about money or bugs all fit in nicely.

## What we'll pass on

We'll close the PR, or take out just that one line, if the phrase:

- is already on the list, even with a small change like a plural or an added `!`
- doesn't actually expand H, C, B (it uses "HCB" itself, or skips a letter)
- needs explaining, like an inside joke or a pun most people won't catch
- is a real person's name or a username, including your own
- has swearing in it, even censored with `*` or `#`
- is crude, or about drugs, violence, or gambling
- pokes fun at a group of people, or could make someone feel singled out
- jokes about robbing or scamming banks (we move real money for real organizations, so that one hits differently coming from us)
- calls HCB a bank (we're not one! the [README](README.md) has the story)
- promotes something, like a link, a product, or an account

Lots of good jokes just aren't homepage jokes. If yours gets passed on, try again!

## Small fixes before merging

To save everyone a round trip, we might push a small fix to your branch before merging, like fixing capitalization or trimming a stray space. If your PR adds a few phrases and one doesn't fit, we'll usually remove that one and merge the rest. We'll always say what we changed.
